Advertising

AlephB uses Google AdSense to show ads on some pages of alephb.uk and its subdomains. Where ads are shown:

  • Cookies: third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Personalised ads: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Opting out: turn off personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, and some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.
  • More: how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Your consent

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, pages with ads show a consent message from Google before any advertising cookie is set.

  • Your choice: accept, refuse, or pick which purposes and vendors you allow.
  • Refusing still lets you use the page; ads may then be non-personalised or limited.
  • Changing your mind: use the privacy and cookie settings link or button on that page, or clear the site's cookies to see the message again.
