  • CompromiseAn iOS app that finds a fair place to meet.

    Meet where the journey is fair for everyone, not just halfway on the map.

    • Private: no account, no ads, addresses stay on your phone.

  • The Ritz AffairA mystery you solve with SQL, in the browser.

    Paris, 1912: the Blue Star sapphire vanishes from the Ritz. You are Inspector Ganimard's clerk, and your only weapon is SQL.

    • A real database of hotel registers, cab rides and telegrams, running in your browser.
    • Twelve chapters that build from a first SELECT up to joins, grouping and CASE, with The Clerk's Handbook explaining every construct.
    • Made for class: investigate alone, or compete as teams on a leaderboard.
    • Nothing to install, no account, free.
