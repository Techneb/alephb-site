The Ritz AffairA game to learn SQL: solve a mystery in the browser.

Paris, 1912: the Blue Star sapphire vanishes from the Ritz. You are Inspector Ganimard's clerk, and your only weapon is SQL. Each chapter teaches one construct, and you need it to find the next clue.

  • 🔎
    Learn by solving a caseTwelve chapters, one construct each. Every query you write turns up a clue.
  • 📖
    The Clerk's HandbookPlain-English lessons on every construct from SELECT to JOIN, GROUP BY and CASE, examples you can run, and twenty graded exercises with solutions.
  • 🏫
    Made for classStudents investigate alone or compete as teams on a live leaderboard. Free, with nothing to install and no account.

What's my size?A Chrome extension that picks your clothing size.

An "M" at one brand is an "L" at another. What's my size? learns from the clothes you already own and tells you which size to pick, right on the shop's product page.

  • 📏
    Your size on the product pageA panel compares the garment with a piece you own that fits, and recommends a size.
  • 👕
    Your wardrobeEvery garment you own, with its photo and how it fits.
  • 🧍
    Your size at each brandA mannequin shows your size for tops, bottoms and shoes, brand by brand.
